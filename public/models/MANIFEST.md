# 3D model manifest

Real items for the wardrobe's contents. Each is a downloaded model of a real object, not modelled for this app.
`scripts/realism/prepare_models.py` brings each one to real size and the app's orientation and reduces it for phones.
Garments modelled as worn (around an invisible body, arms out) are hung on a wooden hanger: the body pressed to half
its worn depth and each sleeve turned down at the shoulder. Their folds' ambient occlusion is baked into vertex colours.

Source: Sketchfab, downloaded through the Objaverse dataset (https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse),
2026-10-12. Licence for all: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
